Short Biography

Currently based in Vertemate Con Minoprio, Italy.

I write/transcribe/arrange/program/perform music.

I am pursuing a PhD in Composition and Performance at Pescara Conservatory.

My research speaks about the aesthetics of production in contemporary music, or: social structures, power and cultural entitlement in post-media art.
